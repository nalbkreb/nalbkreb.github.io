Topic Description

For my course project I have chosen to develop a website based around Historical Eras and Civilizations. Historical eras are artificial constructs used to subdivide history based on similarities found throughout the block. The periods are usually based on the technological and cultural similarities found throughout the block, for instance you can group the Greek city states and Roman civilization together as they existed in close proximity to each other (physically and temporally) and had similar technological levels. You would not group the United States with them.

According to Wikipedia, "A civilization...is any complex society characterized by the development of the state, urbanization, and standardized symbolic systems of communication, namely writing systems." By this definition, we can determine that entities such as the city states of Sumer are a civilization, while societies of hunter-gathers are not.
