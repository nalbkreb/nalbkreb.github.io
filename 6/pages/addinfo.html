Why Was This Site Made?

This site was created as part of Brandon Blankenship's web-dev semester project. The topic was chosen based on his minor.

In addition, this site serves as a jumping off point for those interested in history, but with little direction.

Why These Civilizations?

Each era was, per the project paramaters, to have four civilizations. So for each era, one civilization was chosen from the same geographical region each time. Europe, East Asia, the Americas, and the Near/Middle East.

The specific civilizations chosen were either due to convenience, the abundance of records, or Brandon Blankenship's personal interest.
