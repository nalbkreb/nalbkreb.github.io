Topic Description

For my course project I have chosen to develop a website based around Historical Eras and Civilizations. Historical eras are artificial constructs used to subdivide history based on similarities found throughout the block. The periods are usually based on the technological and cultural similarities found throughout the block, for instance you can group the Greek city states and Roman civilization together as they existed in close proximity to each other (physically and temporally) and had similar technological levels. You would not group the United States with them.

According to Wikipedia, "A civilization...is any complex society characterized by the development of the state, urbanization, and standardized symbolic systems of communication, namely writing systems." By this definition, we can determine that entities such as the city states of Sumer are a civilization, while societies of hunter-gathers are not.

The point of this website will be to provide the user with basic information surrounding various civilizations from different periods of human history, as well as directions to sources to learn more. While there are many websites (Wikipedia, History.com, Brittanica, etc.) that already preform similar functions, this site will act as a jumping off point, allowing the user to choose a specific topic that interests them, learn surface info to confirm their interest, and point them to where they can aquire more detailed information.

App Description

Landing Page

This page will inform the user of the purpose of the website. There will be at least one image consisiting of four seperate images related to the website's content, each taking up one quadrant of the full image. There will also be links to the initial index, two-step, additional info, and contact us pages.

Two-Step

The two step is the section of the project that will ask the user to answer two sequential questions and then provide them unique data based on that selection. In the context of this website the choices will be as follows:

  • First Choice
    • This first choice will prompt the user to pick a historical era (Drawn from here) from the list below. The page itself will consist of a prompt and four buttons each leading to a different set of second choices. Each choice will be accompanied by a short description.
      • Ancient
      • Post-Classical
      • Early Modern
      • Late Modern
  • Second Choice
    • The second choice will be similar in format to the first, however it will prompt the user to choose a civilization to learn about from the period they have selected. As before, each choice will be accompanied by a short description.
      • Ancient
        • Romans
        • Sumerians
        • Chinese
        • Olmecs
      • Post-Classical
        • Spaniards
        • Iranians
        • Koreans
        • Toltecs
      • Early Modern
        • Netherlanders
        • Ottomans
        • Japanese
        • Haudenosaunee
      • Late Modern
        • France
        • Egyptians
        • Vietnamese
        • Guatemalans
  • The Data
    • After making their choices, users will be presented with a data page showcasing the civilization they have chosen. The page will give a brief rundown on the history of the civilization, include at least one image (with all pages having the same number of images) and provide links to external sources for further learning oppurtunities.

Additional Info

This page will provide the user with more info on the project, probably in a similar manner to the topic description above. It will also provide context on why each civilization was chosen for the project.

Contact Us

This page will allow the user to provide the developer with contact information, for feedback purposes. It will collect the user's email adress as well as any questions they may have.
