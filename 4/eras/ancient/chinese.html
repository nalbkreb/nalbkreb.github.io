The Chinese

Pre-Unification Dynasties

Chinese civilization began in the Yellow River valley, around 5000 BC, about the same time many other early human civilizations were emerging. The first dynasties of China, pre her unifications were, in order, the Xia, Shang, and Zhou, though the first two have little evidence of their existance.

Qin Dynasty

In the second century BC, China was unified by the Qin dynasty under its first emperor, Qin Shi Huangdi. This dynasty was short lived however, and China fell apart soon after the emperor's death.

Han Dynasty

After a period of strife, the Han dynasty emerged as the new rulers of China. Under the Han, advances were made in iron, paper, and most importantly, silk, which would become China's major export for centuries to come.
