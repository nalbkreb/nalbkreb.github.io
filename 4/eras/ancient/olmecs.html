The Olmecs

Beginings

The Olmecs were a central american people who flourished in what is today the mexican state of veracruz. They are first doccumented around 1400 BC, later than many other similar civilizations in the old world, but they were no less advanced.

La Venta

After their first major settlment, known to us as San Lorenzo, was abaondoned, the site of La Venta rose to promenence. Found here are great pyramids, pottery, masonry, and carved jade.

Han Dynasty

Unfortunately, during the early half of the third century BC, the Olmec population entered a steep decline and never recovered, leaving the area uninhabited for centuries. It is still unknown as to what caused their downfall.
